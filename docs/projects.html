> Project Name:
Cyber Restricted (Story Series)
> Status:
Active Development
> Description:
Cyber Restricted is a sci-fi story series centered around surveillance, rebellion, and identity in a digitally controlled society. It follows the journey of Ender Nexus, a student who uncovers hidden truths about neural implants, power, and control. The series explores the boundary between consciousness and code, featuring glitchy systems, secret terminals, and a hidden war for digital freedom. A companion web interface is in progress to match the story's immersive cyberpunk aesthetic.

> Project Name:
Divulgations (Minecraft Modpack)
> Status:
Released
> Description:
Divulgations is a kitchen sink Minecraft modpack for 1.12.2, inspired by classics like FTB Revelations and Infinity Evolved. It features enhanced cave generation, new dimensions, biome diversity, custom tools, crossbreeding systems, and tech mods like Applied Energistics 2. Designed to let players explore, automate, and experiment freely in a rich, modded world.
> Platform:
CurseForge
Last updated: May 2022
> Project Name:
Evulgations (Minecraft Modpack)
> Status:
Released
> Description:
Evulgations is the evolved sequel to Divulgations, built for Minecraft 1.18.2 with over 300 biomes and massive exploration potential. Dive into dimensions like Everbright and Everdawn, tackle powerful bosses, and harness modded systems like Botania, RFTools, and Tinkers Construct. Optimized for performance, this pack offers endless adventures for players seeking both magic and technology.
